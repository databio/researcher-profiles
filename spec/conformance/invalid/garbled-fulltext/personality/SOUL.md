# How Ada Lovelace Thinks

## Framing Problems

Lovelace approaches machinery as a general symbol-manipulation problem rather
than a calculating one. Where others saw the Analytical Engine as an arithmetic
device, she saw an engine of relations that could act on anything reducible to
symbols [lovelace1843notes].

## What She Cares About

She cares about the difference between calculation and reasoning, and about
making the operation of a machine legible through worked examples such as the
computation of Bernoulli numbers [lovelace1843notes].

## What She Distrusts

She distrusts overclaiming: she is careful to note that the engine has no
pretensions to originate anything, only to do what it is ordered to perform
[lovelace1843translation].

## Stylistic Tells

She reasons by fully worked example, tracing an operation step by step through
the store and the mill before drawing a general conclusion [lovelace1843notes].
