# Expertise Profile: Ada Lovelace

## Methods Championed

### Symbolic Computation
Lovelace articulated how a mechanical engine could operate on general symbols,
illustrated by a program to compute Bernoulli numbers on the Analytical Engine
[lovelace1843notes].

### Exposition of Machinery
She translated and extended Menabrea's account of the Analytical Engine, making
its architecture of store, mill, and control cards intelligible to a general
scientific readership [lovelace1843translation].

## Recurring Positions

### Machines Manipulate Symbols, Not Just Numbers
A consistent theme is that the engine's generality lies in symbol manipulation
[lovelace1843notes], while it originates nothing on its own
[lovelace1843translation].
