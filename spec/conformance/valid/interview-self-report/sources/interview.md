## Current position and lab
The Vance lab studies how plant cells survive drying. Mara's piece is the
transcriptional side: which genes switch on in the first hours of rehydration.
She reports to Dr. Elena Vance. A typical week is two days at the bench
(RNA extraction, qPCR), two days of analysis in R, and one day of reading and
lab meeting.

## Research questions
1. Which regulators fire first when a dried moss cell rehydrates?
2. Is the recovery program the same across species, or does each lineage
   reinvent it?
3. Can the early recovery program be tuned to make crop plants more tolerant?

## Methods and skills
Confident: RNA extraction, qPCR, RNA-seq library prep, R (tidyverse, DESeq2),
basic Python. Learning: ATAC-seq, Snakemake, statistics beyond linear models.
