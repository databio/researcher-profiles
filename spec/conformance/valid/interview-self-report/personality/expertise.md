# Expertise Profile: Mara Okonkwo

## Methods

Mara works at the transcriptional level of plant stress recovery: RNA
extraction, qPCR, and RNA-seq library preparation at the bench, with analysis
in R (tidyverse, DESeq2) and basic Python [interview]. She is learning
ATAC-seq, Snakemake, and statistics beyond linear models [interview].

## Research questions

Her central question is which regulators fire first when a dried moss cell
rehydrates, followed by whether that recovery program is conserved across
lineages [interview]. A longer-range interest is whether the early program can
be tuned in crop plants, though she is explicit that understanding the biology
comes first [interview].

## Intellectual lineage

Undergraduate work on moss desiccation tolerance with Ruth Alder led to the
Vance lab, where the lab's question (how plant cells survive drying) became her
own narrower question about the first hours of rehydration [interview].

## Recurring critiques

She does not trust a result until she has seen the raw counts, and she is
frustrated that the field publishes gene lists without following any of them
up [interview].

## Career trajectory

Third-year PhD student in the Vance lab at Example University (since 2023),
with one poster and one analysis pipeline to her name so far [interview]. She
wants to run a small lab on stress recovery in non-model plants [interview].
