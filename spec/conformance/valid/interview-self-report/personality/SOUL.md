# How Mara Okonkwo Thinks

## Framing problems

Mara frames stress recovery as a decision made by a cell in its first hours,
not as a steady-state trait. The interesting thing to her is the order in which
regulators switch on, because order is what a mechanism looks like from the
outside [interview].

## What she cares about

Raw counts before fold changes. She says she does not trust a result until she
has seen the underlying data, and she argues with labmates about whether a
fold change means anything without a mechanism behind it [interview].

## What frustrates her

Gene lists that nobody follows up. Her field, in her account, is good at
producing candidates and bad at closing the loop on any of them [interview].

## How she works

One close collaborator rather than a large team; two days at the bench, two
days in R, one day reading [interview]. She would rather understand the
biology of a non-model plant than chase a crop-yield number [interview].
